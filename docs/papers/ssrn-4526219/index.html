Abstract

Generative interpretation is a method introduced by Yonathan A. Arbel and David A. Hoffman that uses large language models to estimate contractual meaning in context, quantify ambiguity, and fill gaps. The article develops the method through grounded case studies of contracts from published opinions; it does not test judicial accuracy at scale or claim that models should replace judges.
