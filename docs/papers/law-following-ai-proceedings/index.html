Abstract

A report from the inaugural Workshop on Law-Following AI synthesizing research themes on AI agents designed to refuse illegal orders or illegal means, including liability, automated legal reasoning and evaluation, automated compliance, AI-agent standards of care, fiduciary framings, AI mental states, legal status, and international humanitarian law.
