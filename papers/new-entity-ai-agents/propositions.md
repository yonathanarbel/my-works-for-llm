## 1. Billions of autonomous, mutable AI agents will create harms that law cannot govern until it can identify which agent acted

**Location:** Opening and Thesis, unpaginated online source

When harm follows, law must answer which AI did it. This is significant because attribution is logically prior to liability or deterrence. **Evidence anchor:** The opening lists current agent actions, predicts proliferating mutable swarms, and asks which AI caused an eventual harm.

**Boundary:** The projection of billions of agents is forward looking, and the essay does not estimate a deployment timeline or prevalence of each harm.
