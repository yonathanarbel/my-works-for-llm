## 1. The effectiveness of consumer-driven market discipline determines how much legal intervention consumer markets require

Professor Yonathan A. Consumers can also help govern market norms, so lawmakers deciding whether and how to intervene need a realistic account of that bottom-up mechanism. If the operative theory identifies the wrong consumers or actions, policy may either overestimate self-correction or displace useful private enforcement. This is significant because descriptive market theory sets the baseline for consumer-protection design.
