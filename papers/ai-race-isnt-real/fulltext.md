# The AI Race Isn’t Real

Canonical page: https://works.battleoftheforms.com/papers/ai-race-isnt-real/

The AI Race Isn’t Real

Canonical citation:
Yonathan A. Arbel & Matthew Tokson, The AI Race Isn’t Real, Lawfare (2026).

Abstract:
Argues that the AI race frame is descriptively flawed and normatively dangerous: AI competition has no finish line, knowledge diffusion weakens durable first-mover advantages, and race dynamics can erode safety, destabilize deterrence, and narrow the set of available policy tools.
